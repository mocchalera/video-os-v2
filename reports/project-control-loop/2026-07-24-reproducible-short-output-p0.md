  output/temp directory creation.
- Checks Node 22, executable startup for ffmpeg/ffprobe, caption filters, and

  that no output directory is created.

### TC-0142 — final caption invariant

- Word timing and semantic timing are followed by one-frame separation,
  persisted dwell/CPS recomputation, then validation.
- Below 300 ms, overlap, stale metrics, unresolved anchors, and premature
  protected reveals are blocking.
- 300–800 ms remains advisory to preserve intentional impact captions.
- Caption approval and package QA revalidate the final artifact.

### TC-0143 — complete final-output scan

- Final media scan uses ffprobe metadata plus `ffmpeg -xerror`.
- Video and optional audio are decoded.
- `out_time_us` and `progress=end` must prove that the scan reached the probed
  duration within one frame.
- The regression suite detects a 0.55 s four-sided inset, a freeze, a partial
  scan, dimension drift, and decode failure.
- Canonical still-image, contain-fit, full-frame CTA, freeze-hold, and
  fade-to-black intent derives reasoned detector exception ranges. Unlabeled
  video derives none.

# Suno Production Briefs — Core Pack v1

## How to use these briefs

These are starting briefs for a human-operated generation session. They do not indicate that any track has been generated.

For every attempt:

1. Use an eligible paid account and keep the result Link-Only/private.
2. Select instrumental generation. Do not enter lyrics, vocal syllables, artist names, band names, producer names, song titles, branded style labels, or reference audio.
3. Paste the track's `Style / prompt` as the creative direction. Add the `Always exclude` block and the track-specific exclusions where the interface permits; otherwise incorporate them as plain negative instructions.
4. Generate at least three materially distinct candidates before selecting. A “variation” must change composition/arrangement, not only mastering.
5. Record the exact prompt, displayed model/version, timestamps, candidate IDs, URLs, plan evidence, and downloads in the private provenance manifest.
6. Treat duration, key, BPM, and structure as targets. Verify the actual audio; never label an unverified generated file with catalog metadata.
7. Do not publish, remix with another user, or represent a candidate as accepted. Human editing, mixing, similarity review, and rights approval remain mandatory.

### Always include

> Original instrumental background music for dialogue-led video. No vocals, spoken words, chants, vocal chops, or pseudo-vocal lead. Keep the midrange uncluttered and leave regular breathing space for narration. Clear 4- or 8-bar phrases, stable tempo, clean bar-line edit points, a sparse clean intro, and a deliberate clean ending. Avoid any resemblance to a known artist, recording, song, film score, game score, advertising cue, or sonic logo. No copyrighted samples or imitated signature sounds.

### Always exclude

Artist or song imitation; recognizable melody; vocals; choir; spoken word; producer tag; watermark; audio-logo motif; dominant lead instrument; unpredictable tempo; rubato; long uneditable intro; abrupt AI artifacts; excessive limiting; dense upper-midrange; constant fills; uncontrolled reverb tails.

## Human handoff after each generation pair

Return the following information before production can continue:

- Track IDs attempted.
- Exact prompts actually used, including edits.
- Displayed Suno model/version and paid plan confirmation at generation time.
- Candidate IDs/private references and generation timestamps.
- Original downloaded files and SHA-256 hashes in the private evidence store.
- Short human notes: strongest candidate, rejected candidates, speech-masking concerns, artifact concerns, and any resemblance concern.

Do not put account name, receipts, contracts, private URLs, or source audio in Git. Once this handoff exists, the next unit is human shortlist review and arrangement planning—not automatic release.
